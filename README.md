Hi, I'm Luca Gianforcaro 👋

I'm a biomedical engineer fascinated by one question: how does raw data come to life?

Sensor streams, joint angles, pixels. On their own they are just numbers. Pass them through the right model and they turn into structure: a recognised movement, a detected defect, a robot arm that knows what to do next. That journey, from noisy signals to meaningful behaviour, is what I love building.

🔭 Where I'm pointing that curiosity

🦾 Physical AI Learned models that leave the screen and act in the real world. I built my own SO101 arm with LeRobot and I'm training imitation learning policies on it, starting in MuJoCo with datasets I collect myself through teleoperation.

👁️ Computer vision beyond robotics I'm developing an early stage startup idea around vision models for industrial quality control: anomaly and defect detection on production lines, adaptable to different manufacturing use cases.

🧠 Human movement My MSc thesis at the BioRobotics Institute (Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna) taught a model to read movement intention from an upper limb exoskeleton, to drive functional electrical stimulation of the hand.

📚 Always learning

Robot learning, reinforcement learning, generative models, and lately Vision Language Action models. Most of what I study ends up as an experiment in one of these repositories.
